### S13 Lead time — field is unusable

`EventAgendaLastPublishedUTC` is the **last** publication time, not the first. Agendas are republished on meeting day and after the meeting, which yields negative and near-zero "lead times":

| Body | n | min h | median h |
|---|---|---|---|
| Guilford Board of Commissioners | 20 | −503.5 | 4.6 |
| Winston-Salem City Council | 9 | −1.7 | 5.2 |

So the API cannot tell us when an agenda first appeared. **Proposed AC9 change:** T is the time our ingest first sees the published agenda. The SLA becomes: the review issue opens in the same ingest run that first sees it, and ingest starts at least every 6 hours (skew logged by S5). Worst case from posting to issue is then 6 h plus cron skew. Real first-publication lead times get measured from our own `first_seen` records once ingest runs.

### CI lessons from this run

- `setup-dotnet` with `10.0.x` (and with a `global.json` using `rollForward: latestPatch`) picked SDK 10.0.403, which was listed but not downloadable (HTTP 409) on Oct 25 and broke every .NET job. **Install an exact SDK version in CI** (`dotnet-version: 10.0.402`) and bump it deliberately.
- GitHub annotates that `ubuntu-latest` is moving to Ubuntu 26 (from Oct 19, 2026). **Pin the runner image** (`ubuntu-24.04`) in production workflows so the OS changes only on purpose.
