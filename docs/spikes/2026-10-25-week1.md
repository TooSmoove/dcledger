### S6 Pinned VTIMEZONE — PASS (cross-check); .NET half pending

- `spikes/vtimezone/america-new_york.vtimezone.ics`: 344 bytes, CRLF, SHA-256 `0c5fc3087bfde95550c1936bbe2d9bd1098a4f93010f7e96bc1830ec60032136`. Rules: DAYLIGHT 2nd Sunday of March 02:00, STANDARD 1st Sunday of November 02:00, no UNTIL.
- Parsed as a VTIMEZONE (python `icalendar` 6.3.2, built from the block itself, not the system database) and compared with IANA `America/New_York` every 15 minutes from 2007 through 2037: **1,087,008 instants, 0 mismatches**.
- Golden instants: Oct 15, 2026 17:30 local → **21:30Z**; Nov 5, 2026 17:30 local → **22:30Z**.
- Finding: libraries disagree on wall times inside the spring-forward gap (e.g. Mar 14, 2027 02:30). **Rule for the domain: the validator rejects meeting times that don't exist (gap) or occur twice (fall-back overlap).** Real meetings never fall there, so rejecting is safer than guessing.
