# Spike S5: GitHub cron skew and the ingest cadence (issue #4)

Measured Nov 8, 2026 from the Actions API (`created_at` of every `schedule` run) for three workflows on this public repository:

- `cron-skew`: an hourly probe at :17, Oct 25 17:25 UTC to Nov 8 20:19 UTC.
- `ingest`: every 6 h at :17, from Oct 29.
- `publish`: every 6 h at :47, from Oct 30.

Each run is assigned to the latest slot at or before its start. A run more than one slot late can't be told apart from a punctual run of a later slot, so delays are lower bounds. The gap between consecutive starts is the number that bounds AC9.

## Results

| Workflow | Slots due | Ran | Skipped | Start delay p50 / p95 / max | Gap between starts p50 / p95 / max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `cron-skew`, hourly at :17 | 339 | 56 | **83%** | 38 / 56 / 60 min | 6.1 / 8.6 / 10.8 h |
| `ingest`, every 6 h at :17 | 41 | 26 | **37%** | 114 / 354 / 355 min | 9.0 / 13.3 / **13.4 h** |
| `publish`, every 6 h at :47 | 39 | 24 | 41% | 73 / 351 / 358 min | 9.0 / 13.1 / 15.0 h |

- **GitHub skips scheduled runs; it doesn't just delay them.** The hourly probe ran 3 to 5 times a day for 14 days, never more. Skipped slots leave no trace in the run list.
- **The 6-hour ingest already misses AC9.** 4 of 25 gaps were over 12 h: Nov 3 01:00 → 14:21 (13.4 h), Nov 4 06:30 → 19:48 (13.3 h), Nov 5 06:52 → 19:59 (13.1 h), Nov 6 06:52 → 19:27 (12.6 h). All four are a dropped 12:17 UTC slot, which leaves no run from early morning to mid-afternoon Eastern on weekdays. 8 of 25 gaps were over 10 h.
- **Run time doesn't matter.** Scheduled ingest runs took 0.5 to 0.9 min of wall time. The two failed runs were manual dispatches during setup on Oct 29.

## Decision

1. **Ingest runs hourly (`17 * * * *`).** The hourly probe's longest gap over 14 days was 10.8 h, inside AC9's 12 h, but with only about 1.2 h of margin and no guarantee. Every 4 h has no data, and the 6 h schedule already misses. Hourly costs little: under a minute per run on a public repository's free minutes, a few R2 operations, and one Legistar listing per run. The per-jurisdiction concurrency group already prevents overlapping runs, and `CronSlot` already parses the hourly expression.
2. **Publish stays every 6 h.** Its dropped runs only delay "last checked", whose stale flag is at 48 h (AC10), and publish also runs after every green `ci` on main. The calendar's `REFRESH-INTERVAL` follows publish and stays `PT6H`.
3. **If hourly still misses 12 h,** the fallback is an external trigger, such as a Cloudflare Worker cron calling `workflow_dispatch`. That needs a token with Actions write access and an ADR, so it waits for evidence: the run log records each run's start, and gaps can be read from the Actions run list the same way as here.

## Cleanup

The probe workflow (`cron-skew.yml`), the week-1 spike workflow (`spikes.yml`) and `spikes/` are removed; nothing else referenced them. Results for those spikes stay in `docs/spikes/`. Issue #1 was already closed.
