### Publisher

NC Data Center Ledger is published by Smoove Software Solutions, a North Carolina LLC based in Greensboro, North
Carolina. One person maintains it.

### Funding

The project has no outside funding. Smoove Software Solutions pays its costs, which are hosting, a domain
and storage.

### Relationships

Smoove Software Solutions has no financial or advisory relationship with any data
center developer or operator, utility, landowner in a recorded case, local government, or advocacy group on these
issues.
